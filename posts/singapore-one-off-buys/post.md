These are not daily goods, but they are where a good buy lasts for years.

Each price has one of three labels. **Page** means the shop's product page was opened on 8 November 2026. **Tracker** means a guide that logs the date it checked. **Listing** means a search result only.

That is S$277 to S$297 in all.

Some notes on these picks:

- The Anker price swings. One Anker store page showed S$39.99 and others S$59.99, so aim for the low end. Other brands of 65W charger were about S$25 to S$45.
- If you only want one or two smart plugs, a single one was S$13.80 on Amazon.sg and S$13.90 at Courts.
- If you want a cheaper air fryer, the Mayer 4.5 L was S$69 on 7 November. The Philips costs S$29 more for the better known brand.
- Courts sells the same trimmer at S$53 with a two-year warranty, but it is on order, so delivery can take longer.

| UGREEN PB312 | 20,000 mAh | 20W | S$33.20 | 602 | Listing, few left |
| UGREEN PB537 | 20,000 mAh | 55W | S$42 | 476 | Page, in stock |
| UGREEN 75173B | 10,000 mAh | 33W | S$33 | 303 | Page |
| Anker Nano A1387 | 20,000 mAh | 30W | S$76.49 | 261 | Listing |
| UGREEN 95670B | 20,000 mAh | 165W | S$87 | 230 | Page |

The PB537 wins. It costs S$8.80 more than the PB312 and has nearly three times the output. If you need to run a laptop, get the 95670B.

Airlines commonly cap power banks at 100 Wh without approval. At 3.7 V, 20,000 mAh is about 74 Wh, so these all fit. Check your airline, as rules differ.
