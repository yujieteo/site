A good post lets the reader move things. This one shows each part of the kit once.

## Footnotes

Hover a number and the note opens.[^1] Tap it and the note stays open.[^2] Press Escape or tap elsewhere to close it.

[^1]: A hover note. It closes when the pointer leaves.
[^2]: A tap note. It stays open until you close it.
