## Before you start

The proof this report covers runs to thousands of pages, and the statement lives in (∞, 1)-categories. The natural feeling is intimidation, a fear that the subject is closed to outsiders. Scholze writes that as a graduate student he could not see what geometric Langlands was about, so the feeling has good company. The introduction states the payoff as a concrete isomorphism of vector spaces. Read those ten pages for pleasure. The other 46 are there if you want them.

## The story

The Langlands programme wants to describe the space of automorphic functions on a locally symmetric space, together with its commuting Hecke operators. The dream answer (Conjecture 1.5) is that this space equals the global sections of the dualising complex on a stack of local systems for the dual group Ǧ. Over number fields nobody can yet define that stack. Over a function field one can: automorphic functions become functions on the 𝔽<sub>q</sub>-points of Bun<sub>G</sub>, the moduli of G-bundles on a curve. The unblocking idea, from Drinfeld, is that such functions are traces of Frobenius on sheaves. So one first proves an equivalence of categories, between sheaves on Bun<sub>G</sub> and coherent sheaves on local systems, and then takes the trace of Frobenius of the whole equivalence.

## Main definitions

**Bun<sub>G</sub> and D(Bun<sub>G</sub>).** For a smooth projective connected curve X over an algebraically closed field k and a reductive group G, Bun<sub>G</sub> is the smooth Artin stack of G-bundles on X. D(Bun<sub>G</sub>) is its category of sheaves in a chosen theory: de Rham (D-modules), Betti or étale.
- In the étale and Betti settings one takes sheaves with nilpotent singular support.

**LocSys<sub>Ǧ</sub>.** The derived stack, over the coefficient field e, of local systems on X for the Langlands dual group Ǧ, in the sense of the chosen sheaf theory.

**Hecke operators.** For a point x of X, the Hecke stack parametrises two G-bundles with an isomorphism away from x. Pull back, tensor with a kernel, push forward. By geometric Satake the kernels are representations of Ǧ, so Rep(Ǧ) acts on D(Bun<sub>G</sub>) for every x.

**IndCoh<sub>Nilp</sub>.** The Ind-completion of coherent complexes on LocSys<sub>Ǧ</sub> with nilpotent singular support. It enlarges the quasicoherent category D<sub>qc</sub>, which is generated by perfect complexes.

## Main results

**Theorem (spectral action; Theorem 2.7).** With a suitable definition of D(Bun<sub>G</sub>), the category D<sub>qc</sub>(LocSys<sub>Ǧ</sub>) acts on D(Bun<sub>G</sub>) and refines the Hecke action. The Hecke operator for a representation V and a point x acts through the vector bundle on LocSys<sub>Ǧ</sub> whose fibre at a local system is the fibre at x of the associated V-local system.
- Meaning: this is the automorphic-to-Galois direction, in categorical form.

**Theorem (geometric Langlands; Theorems 2.11 and 2.12).** If k has characteristic 0, there is an equivalence D(Bun<sub>G</sub>) ≃ IndCoh<sub>Nilp</sub>(LocSys<sub>Ǧ</sub>), linear over D<sub>qc</sub>(LocSys<sub>Ǧ</sub>), in the de Rham, Betti and étale settings. If k has characteristic p larger than a constant depending on G, the same holds with LocSys<sub>Ǧ</sub> replaced by an open and closed substack LocSys′<sub>Ǧ</sub>.
- Step 1: show the three settings are equivalent in characteristic 0, and deduce characteristic p by lifting the curve to Witt vectors.
- Step 2: prove the de Rham case by induction on G, using Whittaker coefficients, Kac–Moody localisation and the geometry of LocSys<sub>Ǧ</sub>.

**Theorem (trace of Frobenius; Theorem 2.2).** For étale ℚ̄<sub>ℓ</sub>-sheaves with the right definition of D(Bun<sub>G</sub>), the natural map from the categorical trace of Frobenius on D(Bun<sub>G</sub>) to the compactly supported functions on Bun<sub>G</sub>(𝔽<sub>q</sub>) is an isomorphism.

**Theorem (Gaitsgory–Raskin, automorphic functions; Theorem 1.7).** Let X be a smooth projective curve over 𝔽<sub>q</sub> and G a reductive group, with p large enough relative to G. Then there is a canonical Hecke-equivariant isomorphism between the compactly supported functions on Bun<sub>G</sub>(𝔽<sub>q</sub>) and the global sections of the dualising complex on an open and closed substack LocSys′<sub>Ǧ</sub> of the stack of ℓ-adic Ǧ-local systems.
- Proof: take the trace of Frobenius on both sides of Theorem 2.12.

## Note, then ignore

- LocSys′ versus LocSys. The substack appears because of a gap in the theory of singular support for étale sheaves in mixed characteristic. One expects equality, and it is known for SL<sub>n</sub>. Safe to skip on a first reading.
- Theta characteristic. A square root of the canonical bundle is fixed only to normalise the equivalence, through the Whittaker sheaf. Safe to skip.
- Two fields. The curve lives over k and the coefficients over e. For D-modules they agree. For function fields k is the algebraic closure of 𝔽<sub>q</sub> and e is ℚ̄<sub>ℓ</sub>. Safe to skip.
- Koszul signs. Geometric Satake needs a sign change in the commutativity constraint. Safe to skip.
- Assumption 1.1. The introduction takes G split and simply connected and Γ = G(ℤ) to keep the number field discussion short. Safe to skip.
