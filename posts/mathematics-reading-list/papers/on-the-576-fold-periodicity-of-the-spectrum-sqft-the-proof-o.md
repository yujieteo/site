slogan: Any ring spectrum that receives the Witten genus the way TMF does cannot repeat sooner than every 576 degrees, and manifolds prove it.
basis: the introduction and the main theorem statements

## Before you start

The title may raise a fear of being out of your depth: spectra, TMF, Anderson duality and supersymmetric field theory in one line. That fear is reasonable, and you can let it be there. The authors themselves tell you to begin with a baby case about KO and the number 8, and it fits in seven pages. If you want to know where a number like 576 can come from in geometry, this is a friendly route. Enjoy the warm-up first. The main case can wait.

## The story

Real K-theory KO repeats every 8 degrees. Topological modular forms TMF repeats every 576 = 24 × 24 degrees. The only known proof of the second fact is a spectral sequence computation, which explains nothing geometric. The authors want a reason. They also believe that a spectrum SQFT classifying two-dimensional supersymmetric field theories exists, but they do not want to assume it equals TMF. So they take any E<sub>∞</sub> ring spectrum with two properties of TMF and prove that it has no invertible element in a degree not divisible by 576. The idea is an integer-valued pairing between classes of SQFT and spin manifolds with string boundary. If Δ<sup>−1</sup> came from SQFT, pairing it with the 4-disc bounded by S<sup>3</sup> would give 1/24. That is not an integer.

## Main definitions

**Periodicity.** For a ring spectrum E, the smallest n > 0 such that π<sub>n</sub>E contains an invertible element, or ∞ if there is none. Multiplying by that element identifies π<sub>k</sub>E with π<sub>k+n</sub>E.

**The assumptions on SQFT (Assumptions 3.1 and 3.8).** SQFT is an E<sub>∞</sub> ring spectrum with E<sub>∞</sub> maps MString → SQFT, SQFT → KO((q)) and Φ from SQFT to rational modular forms. These commute with the Ando-Hopkins-Rezk spin orientation MSpin → KO((q)). Also π<sub>−21</sub>SQFT = 0.
- Consequence: Φ sends π<sub>∗</sub>SQFT into integral weakly holomorphic modular forms ℤ[c<sub>4</sub>, c<sub>6</sub>, Δ, Δ<sup>−1</sup>], with Δ in degree 24.
- TMF satisfies both assumptions.

**Relative Witten genus.** For a spin manifold M with a string structure on its boundary N, a q-series Wit<sub>rel</sub>([M, N]) with rational coefficients. It is defined on π<sub>∗</sub>(MSpin/MString).
- Example: Wit<sub>rel</sub> of the 4-disc with boundary S<sup>3</sup> in its Lie group framing is 1/12 + O(q).

**Anderson duality pairing.** A pairing π<sub>−4k</sub>SQFT × π<sub>4k−20</sub>(MSpin/MString) → ℤ. It comes from a map of spectra SQFT → Σ<sup>−20</sup>I<sub>ℤ</sub>(MSpin/MString), where I<sub>ℤ</sub> is the Anderson dual.
- Formula: the pairing of T with [M, N] is the q<sup>0</sup> coefficient of ½ · Δ · Φ(T) · Wit<sub>rel</sub>([M, N]).

## Main results

**Theorem (lower bound 576; Theorem 1.3 = Theorem 3.11).** Under Assumptions 3.1 and 3.8, π<sub>n</sub>SQFT has no invertible element for n not divisible by 576. So the periodicity of SQFT, possibly ∞, is at least 576.
- Step 1: the invertible elements of the ring of modular forms are ±Δ<sup>d</sup>. So it suffices to show that Δ<sup>d</sup> lies in the image of Φ only if 24 divides d (Proposition 3.13).
- Step 2: for each remaining d, find a spin manifold with string boundary whose pairing with a preimage of Δ<sup>d</sup> would be a non-integer.

**Proposition (the first case; Proposition 1.10).** If kΔ<sup>−1</sup> lies in the image of Φ: π<sub>−24</sub>SQFT → MF<sub>−12</sub>, then 24 divides k.
- Step 1: π<sub>4</sub>(MSpin/MString) ≅ ℤ, generated by the 4-disc with boundary S<sup>3</sup>.
- Step 2: the pairing gives ½ · k · (1/12) = k/24, which must be an integer.

**Theorem (warm-up; Theorem 2.11).** Let SQM be an E<sub>∞</sub> ring spectrum with maps MSpin → SQM → KU compatible with the Atiyah-Bott-Shapiro orientation, and with π<sub>−3</sub>SQM = 0. Then π<sub>n</sub>SQM has no invertible element unless 8 divides n.
- Step: pair with the 2-disc bounded by the circle with its Lie framing. Its relative Dirac index is ½.

## Note, then ignore

- Lower bound only. The paper does not show that an invertible element exists in degree 576. The authors call that an independent problem for a later paper. Safe to skip on a first reading.
- The assumption π<sub>−21</sub>SQFT = 0. The authors admit it has no physical motivation. It serves only to make a certain anomaly map vanish integrally. Safe to skip.
- No Segal-Stolz-Teichner conjecture. The proof never uses a map from SQFT to TMF. This is a design choice, and you can skip the discussion of it.
- Toda brackets in Appendix A. The test manifolds for higher d are built by a manifold-level Toda bracket, guided by known spectral sequence data for tmf. Safe to skip.
- Grading. π<sub>∗</sub> degrees are twice the weight of the modular form, so Δ of weight 12 sits in degree 24. Safe to skip.
