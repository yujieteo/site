## Before you start

This is a long combinatorial paper about cells, faces and horns in the category Θ<sub>2</sub>, with page after page of case checks. The likely feeling is boredom, or dread of the bookkeeping. Either is a fair reaction to this kind of proof. The statements are short and the idea fits in a paragraph. If you want to know what a lax natural transformation is and why it needs its own tensor product, the first two pages tell you. Enjoy those. The case checks can stay unread.

## The story

In a 2-category, a natural transformation can have squares that commute only up to a chosen 2-cell, in a fixed direction. These are lax transformations. Gray found the tensor product that classifies them: 2-functors from A ⊠ B to C are the same as 2-functors from B to the 2-category of 2-functors A → C with lax transformations between them. Street's formal theory of monads uses this. Maehara wants the same tool for (∞, 2)-categories. He works with 2-quasi-categories, which are presheaves on Θ<sub>2</sub>. The definition is quick: apply Gray's product to the building blocks and extend by colimits. Two problems remain. The product must respect weak equivalences, and it is no longer associative on the nose. The paper solves both.

## Main definitions

**Θ<sub>2</sub> and cellular sets.** Θ<sub>2</sub> is the full subcategory of 2-categories on the objects [n; q<sub>1</sub>, …, q<sub>n</sub>]: a chain of n arrows, where the k-th arrow is replaced by a chain of q<sub>k</sub> 2-cells. A cellular set is a presheaf of sets on Θ<sub>2</sub>.

**2-quasi-category.** A fibrant object in Ara's model structure on cellular sets. Its cofibrations are the monomorphisms. 2-quasi-categories are one model for (∞, 2)-categories.

**Gray tensor product of 2-categories, A ⊠ B.** The objects are pairs (a, b). The 1-cells are generated by (f, b) and (a, g). For each pair of 1-cells f and g there is a generating 2-cell γ<sub>f,g</sub> that fills the square, and it need not be invertible.
- It is part of a biclosed monoidal structure on 2-Cat, with internal homs given by lax and oplax transformations.

**Gray tensor product of cellular sets, ⊗<sub>a</sub> (Definition 3.1).** For each a ≥ 0, take the a-ary Gray product of objects of Θ<sub>2</sub>, take its nerve, and extend to all cellular sets so that it preserves colimits in each variable.

## Main results

**Theorem (binary product is left Quillen; Theorem 6.1).** The binary functor ⊗ = ⊗<sub>2</sub> on cellular sets is left Quillen for Ara's model structure.
- Step 1: the pushout-product (Leibniz) version sends two boundary inclusions to a monomorphism (Section 4).
- Step 2: it sends a generating trivial cofibration and a boundary inclusion to a trivial cofibration, by a combinatorial study of cells using "silhouettes" and "cut-points".

**Theorem (associative up to homotopy; Corollary 7.11).** For cellular sets X<sup>1</sup>, …, X<sup>b</sup>, the comparison map from an iterated product ⊗<sub>a</sub>(⊗<sub>b<sub>1</sub></sub>(…), …, ⊗<sub>b<sub>a</sub></sub>(…)) to the flat product ⊗<sub>b</sub>(X<sup>1</sup>, …, X<sup>b</sup>) is built from inner horizontal and inner vertical horn inclusions. So it is a trivial cofibration.
- Step 1: the comparison map is a monomorphism, and its image is the set of "pure" cells (Theorem 7.4).
- Step 2: the remaining cells are attached by filling inner horns.

**Theorem (all arities are left Quillen; Theorem 8.1).** For every a ≥ 1, ⊗<sub>a</sub> is left Quillen. The Leibniz product of monomorphisms is a monomorphism, and it is a trivial cofibration if one of the factors is.

## Note, then ignore

- Lax monoidal, not monoidal. On cellular sets the product is unital up to isomorphism but associative only up to the comparison maps above. That is why every arity is defined separately. Safe to skip on a first reading.
- Why not complicial sets. Verity already built a Gray product for complicial sets. Maehara prefers Θ<sub>2</sub> because both dualities "op" and "co" are easy there. Safe to skip.
- Is it really the Gray product. The author admits that the definition alone does not justify the name, and promises more in later work. Safe to skip.
- Not braided. Already for 2-categories, A ⊠ B and B ⊠ A differ. Taking "op" or "co" swaps the factors. Safe to skip.
- Link to Gaitsgory and Rozenblyum. The main results match two statements that their book uses without proof, in a different model. Safe to skip.
