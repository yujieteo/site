slogan: Motivic sheaf theory is freely generated by one ring stack with a few properties, so a realisation functor needs only one such stack.
basis: the introduction and the main theorem statements

## Before you start

This paper works in (∞, 2)-categories from its first line, and the main theorems contain five adjectives in a row. You may feel shut out by vocabulary you have never used. That is a reasonable reaction. The introduction is written in plain prose and tells the whole plot in four pages. If you want to see why prismatic and de Rham "stacks" keep appearing, this explains the pattern. Read for the idea, and leave the machinery for another day.

## The story

Grothendieck's motives were meant to classify cohomology theories of varieties. A newer aim is to classify coefficient theories instead: sheaf theories with six operations. Scholze proposed a 2-category of 2-motives that receives every such theory. In it a variety X gives an object [X], and sheaves on X × Y give morphisms [X] → [Y]. Separately, Simpson and Drinfeld found that a coefficient theory often comes from one ring stack. For D-modules it is the de Rham stack of the affine line, and you "transmute" every variety through it. Aoki joins the two ideas and proves a precise statement. The 2-category built from stable motivic homotopy theory is the free one on a ring stack satisfying a short list of conditions. All the conditions concern the affine line only.

## Main definitions

**2-category of kernels 2SH(S).** Start from the six-functor formalism SH on quasiprojective S-schemes. Objects are generated by classes [X]. The mapping category from [X] to [Y] is SH(X × Y), and composition is convolution: pull back to X × Y × Z, tensor, push forward with compact support.
- Identity on [X]: the pushforward of the unit along the diagonal.

**Stack and ring stack over a 2-category C.** A stack over a presentably symmetric monoidal 2-category C is an object of CAlg(C)<sup>op</sup>. A ring stack is a functor from polynomial rings ℤ[T<sub>1</sub>, …, T<sub>n</sub>] to CAlg(C) that preserves finite coproducts.
- Reading: R plays the role of the affine line, with its addition and multiplication.

**Sutured, homologically trivial, smooth.** R is sutured if R<sup>×</sup> → R ← {0} gives a recollement, so R is glued from its units and its zero point. It is homologically trivial if, for f: R → point, the counit f<sub>♮</sub>f\* → id is an equivalence. A sutured R is smooth if it is suave and f<sup>!</sup>1 is invertible.
- Equivalent to smooth, for sutured R: f\* has a linear left adjoint f<sub>♮</sub>, and f<sub>♮</sub>s<sub>\*</sub> is invertible for the zero section s (Proposition 4.30).

**Absolute value.** An absolute value on a ring stack R is a multiplicative map N: R → [0, ∞) with N(0) = 0, the triangle inequality, N<sup>−1</sup>(0) = {0} and N<sup>−1</sup>((0, ∞)) = R<sup>×</sup>.
- A ring stack with an absolute value is automatically sutured.

## Main results

**Theorem (universal property; Theorem A).** For a divisorial noetherian scheme S, the functor X ↦ [X] into 2SH(S) is universal among symmetric monoidal functors to stable presentably symmetric monoidal 2-categories that satisfy four axioms.
- Axioms (i) and (ii): smooth pullback has a left adjoint with base change, and a closed subscheme with its open complement gives a recollement.
- Axioms (iii) and (iv): 𝔸<sup>1</sup>-invariance, and invertibility of the Tate twist f<sub>♮</sub>s<sub>\*</sub>.

**Theorem (ring stack form; Theorem B).** The stable presentably symmetric monoidal 2-category 2SH(ℤ) is freely generated by a homologically trivial smooth sutured ring stack.
- Reading: a symmetric monoidal 2-functor out of 2SH(ℤ) is the same as one such ring stack in the target.

**Theorem (étale version; Theorem C).** 2SH<sub>ét</sub>(ℤ) is freely generated by a ring stack as in Theorem B that is also Kummer and Artin–Schreier.
- Kummer: x ↦ x<sup>l</sup> on R[1/l]<sup>×</sup> is a cover for each prime l.
- Artin–Schreier: x ↦ x<sup>p</sup> − x on R/p is a cover for each prime p.

**Theorem (analytic version; Theorem D).** The 2-category 2D<sub>mot</sub>(ℤ; 𝕊) of Berkovich motives with spherical coefficients is freely generated by a Kummer–Artin–Schreier, homologically trivial, smooth ring stack with an absolute value whose open unit disc is homologically trivial.
- Application (Theorem 1.7): the analytic Habiro stack and the Hyodo–Kato stack determine realisation functors.

## Note, then ignore

- "n-category" means (∞, n)-category. The paper fixes this in its first sentence. Safe to take on trust at first.
- Presentable 2-categories. Making "the 2-category of linear categories" precise needs foundations from Stefanich and from Aoki's earlier paper. Safe to skip.
- Choice of schemes. Theorem A uses quasiprojective schemes, but Remark 1.3 says other reasonable classes give the same answer. Safe to skip.
- Spherical coefficients. Scholze defined Berkovich motives with ℤ coefficients. Theorem D uses the sphere spectrum 𝕊. Safe to skip on a first reading.
- 2-rigidity. Theorem 1.10 is announced with its proof deferred to a later paper. Safe to ignore here.
