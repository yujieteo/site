The files are in public/play/beamdiag/fonts/. sans.ttf and serif.ttf are the site's own fonts, made static with fontTools
instancer and cut to their own glyphs with its subsetter (no layout tables, TrueType outlines for PDF embedding).
math.otf (sha256 63e10b5f90945ab508749d4ac52abdaaf9a6b57342990f557ce122f873c36818) covers the mathematical operators, Greek,
arrows and alphabets the site fonts lack. It is cut with hb-subset (harfbuzz 13.2.1).
