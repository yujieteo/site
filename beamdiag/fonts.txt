The files are in public/play/beamdiag/fonts/. sans.ttf and head.ttf are the site's own font, made static with the fontTools 4.61.1
instancer and cut with its subsetter to the characters of the previous sans.ttf (no layout tables, TrueType outlines for PDF
embedding). The cmap maps each character to its cv01 and ss03 glyph, as the site sets them. head.ttf is for the report headings,
at the weight of the page headings.
math.otf (sha256 63e10b5f90945ab508749d4ac52abdaaf9a6b57342990f557ce122f873c36818) covers the mathematical operators, Greek,
arrows and alphabets the site fonts lack. It is cut with hb-subset (harfbuzz 13.2.1).
