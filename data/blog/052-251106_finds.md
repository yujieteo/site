## Keller’s three fundamental computing paradigms

CPU: add, multiply, load, store, compare, branch (nothing can be known about anything), GPU: add, multiply, load, store (when things happen is known, but the addresses aren’t) DSP: add, multiply (everything is known except the data)

## Power costs and data locality

The key is data locality. Having to move data costs power. Instruction set and compiler, and while it’s complex, that complexity is only up front to compile the model. After that, it’s way simpler (aka, less power and less issues) at runtime than CUDA and PyTorch. The only questionable choice is why not take this further? The interconnects between the chips cost power.
