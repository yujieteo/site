## Mathematics and geometry

- Trigonometry is representation theory of $U(1)$.
- On a good cover, sheaf cohomology can be computed by Čech cohomology because the finite intersections are contractible.
- Compact diagonal operators are precisely those whose diagonal entries tend to zero. [Discussion](https://math.stackexchange.com/questions/173073/why-are-compact-operators-small)
- Jack Morava's [Cosmic Galois groups](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1108.4627) connects homotopy theory, number theory, and mathematical physics.
- Cohomology classes can be viewed as homotopy classes of maps into deloopings such as $K(A,n)$. Principal bundles are cocycles; connections transport fibres along paths; gauge transformations are their equivalences. [Cohomology](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/cohomology), [principal bundles](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/principal+bundle), [connections](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/connection+on+a+bundle), [gauge transformations](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/gauge+transformation)
- [Descent](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/descent) asks when compatible local data comes from a unique global object; an [equivariant structure](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/equivariant+structure) is descent to an action groupoid.
- The Yoneda viewpoint treats a space through all its probes and restriction maps. [Motivation for sheaves and higher stacks](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/motivation+for+sheaves%2C+cohomology+and+higher+stacks)

## Physics and field theory

- Fermionic antisymmetry is encoded by anticommuting geometry: repeated states vanish, reflecting the Pauli exclusion principle.
- Independent, Gaussian, and noninteracting systems are the free cases; this is where parallel decomposition is most natural.
- [Gauge theories](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/gauge+theory) describe field configurations as differential-cohomology cocycles, with vector bundles and connections as basic examples.
- [Deformation quantisation](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/deformation+quantization) studies families of noncommutative algebras parametrised by admissible values of Planck's constant.
- The [cobordism hypothesis](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/cobordism+hypothesis#ForNoncompactCobordisms) says framed cobordism is freely generated by a fully dualisable object. Noncompact variants lead to Calabi–Yau objects; a closed TQFT is determined by its value on circles. A [conformal field theory](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/conformal+field+theory) is a functor on conformal cobordisms.

## Systems and strategy

- A [mutex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lock_(computer_science)) prevents concurrent threads from modifying shared state at the same time.
- [Latency numbers every programmer should know](https://github.com/donnemartin/system-design-primer#latency-numbers-every-programmer-should-know): writes cost more than reads, locality matters, compression can be cheap, queues saturate, and remote calls add failure modes.
- John Boyd's [Destruction and Creation](https://cdn.mises.org/destruction_and_creation_by_john_r_boyd.pdf) moves from mismatches and exceptions to decomposition and novel synthesis. Chaos favours superior orientation.
