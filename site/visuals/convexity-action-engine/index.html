Ruin first, then payoff shape. Each action is screened for extreme, irreversible downside, then compared on reliable upside (harvest), right-tail upside (optionality), timing and opportunity cost against alternatives available now. Every number here is an author judgement or a model transform of judgements, on ordinal 0–4 scales. No empirical dataset could be attached in this build, so the planned sources (O*NET, time-use surveys, health reviews) are listed as not retrieved. 395 canonical actions and 26 actions to avoid expand to 14,080 contextual instances (action × manner × timing). That is short of a 10,000-action canonical ontology. Click any number to see where it came from.

Method, assumptions and what is not here
  • Every fact about an action (durations, costs, benefits, right tail, downside, reversibility, decay, regret, timing curves) is an ordinal author judgement on a 0-4 scale. None is measured. Each is labelled JUDGEMENT on the page, with whether it is a category prior or an action-specific estimate.
  • No external dataset was retrieved in this build: O*NET, the American Time Use Survey, the OECD time-use database, Singapore time-use data and health reviews are listed as planned sources, without links, because the build environment could not reach them. Typical-time curves are judged approximations of time-use patterns, not fitted to data.
  • The ontology has 395 canonical actions and 26 actions to avoid. The 14,080 contextual instances are those actions crossed with at most one manner (duration, company or place) and one timing; they are not distinct canonical actions, so the 10,000-canonical-action target is not met.
  • Extreme-downside probabilities are word labels (very low, low, unknown, moderate), never numbers. Unknown is shown as unknown, not as safe. The repeated-exposure chart uses a per-exposure probability you choose, clearly marked as hypothetical.
  • Singapore context (daylight about 07:00-19:15, midday heat 11:00-16:00, typical opening hours, lightning) is a separate adjustment on top of the global prior and can be switched off. Live weather is not known; outdoor actions say so.
  • Class scores (harvest, optionality, compounding, maintenance, recovery, exploration, fragility), time fit and lens rankings are MODEL transforms of the judged facts, and inherit their low confidence. Model thresholds in the sensitivity view show direction, not precision.
  • Decision history is stored only in this browser. It is shown with its sample size and is never used to change rankings.
