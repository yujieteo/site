    Buy the Defence, Check the Luck

    View Markdown

    2026-09-29 · 1 min read

    A slide deck on Fantasy Premier League after Gameweek 5 of 2026/27. It argues that defenders are where the cheapest points are, and that the clubs behind the best-value defenders have been conceding far fewer goals than their chances against would predict, so some of that value is borrowed luck. It runs about fourteen minutes and ends on the four value defenders and how to check them.

    Open the deck full screen. Use → and ← to move between slides, Home and End to jump, F for full screen, and ? for the other keys.

    The figures come from the official Fantasy Premier League API (the bootstrap-static and fixtures endpoints), fetched on 2026-09-28. The expected goals visualization shows the player-level version of the same question.
