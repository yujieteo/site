What I Learned from Others

Learning

What I learnt from my parents

I have learned to not impose ideals onto other people. They were responsible for what they do. They sought independence from what other people think. They valued tradition and routine in hope of a modest life.

What I learnt from my teachers

I have learned to be neat and tidy. They valued consistency and showing up regularly. They taught me to think clearly. They valued hard work outside of writing. Humility without nitpicking was one of their core ideals. I have learned the importance of being organised and using the appropriate techniques for the appropriate work. I have also appreciated good, clear, and condensed expository writing.

What I learnt from my few friends

I learned how to appreciate confidentiality, how to give and take offense, how to stand up for oneself. I have learned when to stay silent before speaking. The diversity of talents I have come the value and enjoy in people is thanks to them.

What I have learnt from other people

From the people who think differently from me and chose a different path in life: I have learned that diversity of thought is absolutely natural. The health of a society arises from the diversity of life on the common basis of a consensus of emergent morality. It is with this I understand from them that everybody concentrates on different things. This forms the colour of much in our world, from which I am lucky to live better than kings back then by virtue of their diligent work in pursuit of goodness in particular parts of the world.
