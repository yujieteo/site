A slide deck on Fantasy Premier League after Gameweek 5 of 2026/27. It argues
that defenders are where the cheapest points are, and that the clubs behind the
best-value defenders have been conceding far fewer goals than their chances
against would predict, so some of that value is borrowed luck. It runs about
fourteen minutes and ends on the four value defenders and how to check them.

`?` for the other keys.
