    Recovery

    Adapted from Alan Couzens, Serious Recovery for Serious Athletes.

    train → cool down → rehydrate and refuel → hydrotherapy
      → proper meal → unload → nap/rest → sleep

    Constants

    Daily targets

    Day Activity Calories
    Weekday 1 h walk + 1 h swim 2,100–2,400
    Weekend 2.5 h walk + 1 h row + 1 h swim 2,500–2,900
    Rest / illness No planned training 1,800–2,100 
    protein floor          120 g/day
useful protein range   130–160 g/day
proper meal            30–45 g protein
sleep opportunity      7.5–9 h

    These are starting targets. Exercise is already included; do not add watch calories to the food budget.

    Lunch and milkshake rule

    Logged lunch Banana Cream Other calories
    ≤650 kcal Yes Banana before swimming allowed if hungry
    650–800 kcal Yes No other caloric snack or drink
    800–1,000 kcal Optional Likely near or above 2,400 kcal
    >1,000 kcal No Keep the fixed dinner

    Example:

    350 breakfast + 70 100PLUS + 650 lunch
+ 500 Banana Cream + 700 dinner
= 2,270 kcal

    The shake fits when it is budgeted; the variable lunch determines whether it fits.

    Conservative lunch logs:

    Lunch Log
    Fish soup + rice 650
    Dry mee kia 650
    Stuff'd Chicken Daily Bowl 700
    Rice, meat and vegetables 750
    Fried rice / noodles 850
    Creamy, fried or restaurant meal 1,000–1,200

    Sequence

    1. Morning walk — 60 minutes. Drink 320 mL 100PLUS during or immediately after the walk because of the heat; log 70 kcal. Add water to thirst.
    2. Breakfast at home. Eat porridge + egg; log 350 kcal. Log 450 if the serving is visibly large, oily or meat-heavy.
    3. Lunch. Log it conservatively and use the table above to decide the milkshake.
    4. Before swimming. If genuinely hungry and lunch was more than five hours ago, eat one banana; otherwise drink water.
    5. Swim — 60 minutes total. Use roughly 50 minutes controlled work + 10 minutes very easy cooldown.
    6. Rehydrate. Drink 500–750 mL water over the next hour; shower and change.
    7. If budgeted, drink Banana Cream. Have it after swimming, not immediately before.
    8. Dinner. Eat the fixed Stuff'd bowl within about one hour of finishing.
    9. Unload. Spend 20–30 minutes off the feet; elevate the legs if tired. No second workout or dinner.
    10. Sleep. Allow 7.5–9 hours.

    Weekend

    Budget

    With Banana Cream included:

    variable breakfast                 500–800
morning 320 mL 100PLUS                  70
Botak lunch                            600
between-session fuel               200–250
Banana Cream                           500
Stuff'd dinner                         700
------------------------------------------
planned total                     2,570–2,920 kcal

    Banana Cream therefore fits by default when breakfast is about 500–750 kcal. With an 800 kcal breakfast the day lands near the top of the range. If breakfast exceeds 900 kcal, skip the shake or deliberately accept a higher-calorie day; do not remove lunch, training fuel or dinner.

    Training sequence

    1. Breakfast before the walk — 500–800 kcal. Aim for 20–35 g protein and 60–100 g carbohydrate.
    2. Walk — 2 hours 30 minutes. Drink the fixed 320 mL 100PLUS = 70 kcal during the hottest portion and another 500–1,000 mL water across the walk. Add electrolytes or water in heavier heat.
    3. Lunch at OTH. Eat Botak sliced fish porridge + egg; log 600 kcal. Wait 60–90 minutes before rowing if possible.
    4. Row — 60 minutes total. Use roughly 50 minutes work + 10 minutes easy cooldown; drink 300–500 mL water.
    5. Between sessions. Eat one banana and drink 500 mL 100PLUS; log 200–250 kcal. This is light, rapidly available fuel. Do not substitute the heavy milkshake here.
    6. Swim — 60 minutes total. Use roughly 45 minutes controlled work + 15 minutes very easy cooldown. The longer cooldown begins the strict recovery sequence below.

    Weekend Couzens recovery sequence

    Couzens wrote his full protocol for a key endurance session above roughly 150 TSS. The weekend walk + row + swim is the day on which to apply its structure most closely.

    Time Couzens principle OTH implementation
    T−15 to T+00 15-minute cooldown below ~60% maximum heart rate Final 15 minutes of the swim are very easy
    T+00 Begin carbohydrate, protein and fluid immediately Start 500–750 mL water; shower and change
    T+15–45 Cold-water immersion or contrast work while sipping recovery drink The easy pool cooldown supplies immersion; if practical, remain in the water moving easily for another 5–10 minutes
    T+30–45 Recovery drink providing carbohydrate and some protein Good Nectar Banana Cream; log 500 kcal
    T+45–75 Change, compress and continue feeding Eat the fixed Stuff'd bowl within about one hour; this supplies the reliable protein component
    T+75–95 Return home and unload Travel home; sit or lie down; elevate legs; compression is optional
    T+95–115 20 minutes self-massage Gentle self-massage or foam rolling
    T+115–135 20 minutes supine yoga Easy floor mobility; no loaded stretching
    T+135–155 20 minutes meditation / progressive relaxation Quiet breathing or eyes-closed rest
    T+155–245 1–2 hour nap with legs elevated Nap 60–90 minutes if the session ends early enough; otherwise preserve nighttime sleep
    After nap Second carbohydrate/protein feeding Only if hungry or the calorie target permits: yogurt + fruit, milk + banana, or eggs + toast, 250–350 kcal
    Evening Hearty dinner, heat, early bedtime Stuff'd already counts as dinner; warm shower or bath; low stimulation; bed before roughly 21:30 when practical

→ immediate fluid and carbohydrate
→ immersion
→ protein-containing meal
→ elevation / compression
→ massage
→ supine mobility
→ relaxation
→ nap
→ second feed only when needed
→ heat and early sleep

    If a second recovery feed is required, add 250–350 kcal and accept a day near maintenance rather than deleting the protein meal.

    Adjustment

    Judge the system by the seven-day weight average, hunger, sleep and training quality.

    average falling 0.3–0.8 kg/week
→ change nothing

no fall for 2 complete weeks
→ remove 150–200 kcal/day
→ remove optional snacks/add-ons first

falling >1% of body mass/week,
persistent hunger, poor sleep or declining training
→ add 150–250 kcal/day
→ prefer carbohydrate around training or lean protein

    Logging

    unknown meal          use upper half of plausible range
visible extra oil     add 100–200 kcal
creamy sauce          add 100–250 kcal
sweet drink           log separately
exercise estimate     do not eat it back automatically

    The rule

    weekday lunch determines whether Banana Cream fits
weekend Banana Cream follows the swim

weekday dinner = Stuff'd
weekend lunch  = Botak sliced fish porridge + egg
weekend dinner = Stuff'd

    Calories for customised local meals are conservative working estimates rather than i published laboratory values. Adjust only from the two-week trend, not from one meal or one scale reading.
