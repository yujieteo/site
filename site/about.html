Values

These are not qualities I possess perfectly. They are things I try to return to when deciding how to live and work.

  • Focus. Choose a small number of things that matter and keep returning my attention to them.
  • Health. Protect the physical and mental stability that makes everything else possible.
  • Thoughtful openness. Listen seriously to other people without giving up the responsibility to think for myself.
  • Speaking when it matters. Important things should be said clearly, even when I am inclined to remain quiet.
  • Adaptability. Change my mind, plans, and methods when circumstances or evidence change.
  • Respect. Try to understand people before judging them and treat their perspectives as real.
  • Generosity. Share useful knowledge, attention, and help when I can.
  • Forward movement. Take the next concrete step instead of waiting to become a different kind of person.
