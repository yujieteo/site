Lullapixie Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Summary

Lullapixie does not collect personal data. There is no account, no server, no ads and no tracking. Everything stays on your device.

Your photos and paintings

When you choose a photo, the app turns it into a grid of numbered colours on your device. The app does not keep the original photo and never uploads it. Your paintings stay on your device. You can export a painting file and keep it where you choose, for example in iCloud Drive through the Files app.

Photos access

The app uses the system photo picker, so it sees only the photos you pick. When you tap Save, it asks for "add only" access to add your video, GIF, image or wallpaper to your photo library. It cannot read your library.

Your nickname

The app can ask for a nickname to write in the caption of your videos. It is optional. It is stored only on your device, and it leaves your device only inside a video that you choose to share. Use a nickname, not your real name.

Purchases and tips

Studio is a one-time purchase, and the "Feed Lullapixie" treats are optional tips. Apple handles every payment through the App Store. The app checks your purchases on your device with StoreKit. We do not receive your payment details or your name.

Glow chain and notifications

The glow chain (the days you painted) is stored only on your device. If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules one notification a day on your device. Nothing is sent to us.

No analytics

The app has no analytics, no tracking and no crash-reporting tools. It contains no code that sends data over the internet. If you turn on "Share With App Developers" in your iPhone settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports with us. You control this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the iOS share sheet sends your file to the app you choose. That app's own privacy policy then applies.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be on this page with a new date.
