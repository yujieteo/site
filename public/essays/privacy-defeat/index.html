Take Torvalds' take on privacy

31 October 2026

I give up and just suck it up and take Torvald's take on privacy, be so uninteresting that nobody cares if they see inside your home or whatever, see his Linus Tech Tips video.

Too hard to keep with all the spying, not saying that one shouldn't care about enterprise privacy, but personal privacy feels basically impossible unless you just airgap everything you do. Escape from that fear is much more feeling.

Torvalds also says he tries to be so public that he poisons the well, not sure what he means, either way I do not advocate this approach, this is too tiring. We already live defeated in a spyware dystopia. Intel Management Engine contains proprietary code deep in every computer, just have to believe Intel that it is all OK.

Security depends on trust more than we admit. Epstein used Gmail for years and just did not care when he could have had a private arrangement. His emails did become public, but his choice makes me wonder, can we even have faith in crazy hard cybersecurity, just trust Google and keep a low profile. I guess the conclusion is that the panopticon makes you feel so defeated you concede. At least I feel my own precautions don't make much difference somehow.
