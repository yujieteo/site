Lullapixie Support

Contact: contact@teoyujie.org. We reply within 2 business days.

How do I paint my own image?

Tap New painting, choose an image from Photos or Files, choose a size and colours, then tap Create. Select a number at the bottom and drag your finger over the glowing cells. Tap once to fill a whole area.

How do I share?

In a painting, tap the share button. Choose Video, GIF, Image or Wallpaper, then Share. If the app you want is not in the share sheet, tap Save and upload the file from that app. Export is free and unlimited.

What is Studio? Is it a subscription?

No. Studio is a one-time purchase. You pay once and keep it. It adds the Ultra and Super Ultra sizes, all colours and finishes, piano and guitar sounds, the daily fractal, unlimited paintings, and your own caption in videos and GIFs.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account that you bought with.

How do I ask for a refund?

Apple handles refunds. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

What is the glow chain?

Paint 30 cells on a day and Lullapixie the baby bird pixie lights that day. Rest days keep your chain safe when you miss a day. Missing a day never deletes your paintings.

Who made Lullapixie?

Lullapixie was made with help from Claude, an AI model by Anthropic. All art is drawn in code. The app has no AI features and sends no data.

Accessibility

Lullapixie works with VoiceOver, Voice Control and Switch Control: double-tap the painting to paint the next area. The brush is large, and one tap fills a whole area. Settings › Accessibility has Bold numbers. Dynamic Type, Reduce Motion, Bold Text, Increase Contrast and dark mode are supported.

How do I use a painting as my wallpaper?

In the share screen choose Wallpaper, then tap Save. Open the picture in Photos, tap Share, then tap Use as Wallpaper. iOS lets only you set a wallpaper, so this last step is in Photos.

What is "Feed Lullapixie"?

An optional tip jar in Settings. A treat is a tip: it unlocks nothing, and Lullapixie says thank you with a small heart.

Privacy

The app collects no data. Read the privacy policy.

How do I back up a painting?

Touch and hold a painting in the gallery and choose Share file. To bring it back, choose Open file in the gallery menu.
