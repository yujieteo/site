Marklove Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Summary

Marklove does not collect your data. Marklove has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your projects stay on your device.

Your projects

Your projects are on your device: the Markdown file, images, audio, video and the version history (Git). Marklove does not send your projects to us or to other companies. On iPhone and iPad, your projects show in the Files app, and your device backups can include them, as your backup settings say. Exports, ZIP files and shared files go only where you send them, for example with the Share button.

Settings

Marklove keeps your settings on your device, for example the theme, the text size and the find syntax.

Photos and files

When you add a photo, the system photo picker opens. Marklove gets only the photos that you select. It cannot see your photo library. When you import a file, Marklove gets only the file that you select.

Narration voice

The narration voice (Kokoro-82M) works on your device. Your text does not leave your device for narration. The voice model is not in the app. When you select Download the Voice, Marklove downloads the model from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). For this download, Hugging Face gets the network data of a normal web request, for example your IP address. Marklove sends no personal data and no project content. The privacy policy of Hugging Face applies to this request. The model is not in your device backups, and you can delete it in Settings › Narration.

Purchases

Marklove Pro is a one-time purchase. Apple processes every payment through the App Store. Marklove checks your purchase on your device with StoreKit. We do not get your payment details or your name.

Links

When you open a link in a document, the link opens in your browser. The privacy policy of that website applies.

No analytics

Marklove has no analytics, no tracking and no crash-reporting tools. If you turn on "Share With App Developers" in the settings of your device, Apple can share anonymous crash reports with us. You control this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Beta tests with TestFlight

If you test a beta version with TestFlight, Apple can send us crash reports, usage data and the feedback that you send. If we invited you by email, we also see your name and email address. The TestFlight terms and the privacy policy of Apple apply.

Children

Marklove collects no data from anyone, also not from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be on this page with a new date.
