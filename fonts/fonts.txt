scripts/fonts.sh verifies each upstream SHA-256 before it cuts a subset with HarfBuzz 14.5.1
(static instances, no hinting, only the layout features the pages use), then renames two subsets
with fontTools 4.65.0. Two runs give identical bytes.

The faces stand in for the ones the design names, which have no free licence. Each stand-in is the
oldest design that is still in continuous use and has a free version:
- Didot (1784) for headings: Bodoni (1798), as Bodoni Moda. GFS Didot was rejected: its Latin
  letters are Palatino.
- Bembo or Garamond for text: Garamond (c. 1540), as EB Garamond, from the 1592 Egenolff-Berner
  specimen. Bembo's model (1495) went out of use until 1929.
- Akzidenz-Grotesk (1898) for the interface: Franklin Gothic (1902), as Libre Franklin.
- News Gothic (1908) for small text: News Gothic itself, as News Cycle, from the 1908 specimens.
- MathTime Pro II or Times New Roman for maths: STIX Two Math, the open Times-style maths font.
  TeX Gyre Termes Math is closer to Times but is only published on CTAN.
- Courier Prime for code: Courier Prime.

All six families use the SIL Open Font License; the licence files are beside the fonts. News Cycle
and STIX Two Math have Reserved Font Names, so their subsets are named Site Small and Site Math.
