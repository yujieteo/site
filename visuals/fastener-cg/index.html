Fastener Pattern CG Tracker

Place a fastener group on the canvas or in the table. The page reports its shear, axial and area centroids, polar moment J, Ixx, Iyy, Ixy and principal axes, reduces a general 3D load applied anywhere to the group, and shares it among the fasteners by the elastic method. Key in shear and tension allowables to get each fastener's exact load-scale-factor margin of safety under a shear-tension interaction, and the critical fastener. Every number is recalculated as you edit.

Preliminary sizing — verify against the governing specification. The tool ships no allowables and follows no single design code.

    Methods

    Group defaults

    Fasteners use these unless a table cell overrides them (bold). Clearing a cell returns it to the default. The tool ships no allowables: a check without one is not evaluated.

    Shear-tension interaction

    IF(k) = (k·Rs / Fs)a + (k·Rt / Ft)b; MS = k* − 1 where IF(k*) = 1.

    MS is the exact multiplier on the whole applied load at which IF reaches 1. With (a, b) ≠ (1, 1) it differs from 1/IF − 1 (W-006); the plain IF(1) is shown beside it.

    Prying and preload

    b' = b − D/2, a' = min(a, 1.25b) + D/2, ρ = b'/a', δ = 1 − d_h/p, t_c = √(4Bb'/(pFp)), α' = (1/δ)[(T/B)(t_c/t)² − 1] ∈ [0, 1], Q = Bδα'ρ(t/t_c)²

    Flange thickness t and flange strength Fp come from the loaded plate (Plates card). A manual factor f makes the bolt tension f·T (W-012).

    F_b = max(P_max + φT, T) + Q; clamp = P_min − (1 − φ)T; separation at T = P_min/(1 − φ)

    Torque calculator, T = K·D·P

    φ is required and has no default. Friction slip is not evaluated (N-005).

      Results

      Verification

      Runs the hand-calculation cases and property tests from the specification on this page's own solver. The same cases run in the repository's test suite.

      Method

      • Three centroids, always reported separately: shear Cs (weighted by ks; the headline CG), axial Ca (weighted by ka) and area Cg (weighted by A).
      • J = Σ ks(u² + v²) about Cs. Ixx = Σ ka q², Iyy = Σ ka p², Ixy = Σ ka p q about Ca. Principal values I₁,₂ = (Ixx + Iyy)/2 ± √(((Ixx − Iyy)/2)² + Ixy²); θp = ½ atan2(−2Ixy, Ixx − Iyy), CCW from +x to the I₁ axis. Section properties are in length² × weight.
      • Load reduction from P = (xp, yp, zp) to Q on z = 0 with r = P − Q: Mx,Q = Mx + ry Fz − zp Fy; My,Q = My + zp Fx − rx Fz; Mz,Q = Mz + rx Fy − ry Fx. Shear and torsion reduce to Cs, axial load and bending to Ca.
      • Elastic in-plane shear: direct Rd = F ks/Σks; torsional Rtx = −Mz,s ks v / J, Rty = +Mz,s ks u / J.
      • Axial method (a), rigid plate with the neutral axis through Ca: D = Ixx Iyy − Ixy², θx = (Iyy Mx,a + Ixy My,a)/D, θy = (Ixy Mx,a + Ixx My,a)/D, T = ka(Fz/Ka + θx q − θy p). Negative T is unloading (clamp-up), shown as computed.
      • Interaction: IF(k) = (k Rs/Fs)^a + (k Rt/Ft)^b with separate exponents a and b (presets: elliptical (2, 2), the default, and linear (1, 1)). Rt is the positive tension; unloading counts as zero (N-006). The margin is the exact load scale factor, MS = k* − 1 with IF(k*) = 1, found by a bracketed Brent search (tolerance 1e-12 on k, 200 iterations); a failed solve shows “MS not computed” (W-008) and never a substitute number. For a = b = 1 it equals 1/IF − 1. The governing MS of a fastener is its lowest margin across modes, and the critical fastener has the lowest governing MS.
      • Prying (T-stub, keyed B and Fp, no code factor built in) applies only to positive external tension T: a is limited to 1.25·b; b' = b − D/2, a' = a + D/2, ρ = b'/a', δ = 1 − d_h/p, t_c = √(4Bb'/(pFp)), α' = (1/δ)[(T/B)(t_c/t)² − 1] clamped to [0, 1], Q = Bδα'ρ(t/t_c)². t and Fp are the loaded plate's thickness and flange strength. A manual amplification factor f replaces the T-stub with a bolt tension f·T (W-012).
      • Preload P_max, P_min and load-sharing factor φ (required, 0 < φ < 1): F_b = max(P_max + φT, T) + Q, clamp force P_min − (1 − φ)T, separation load P_min/(1 − φ). Once clamp force reaches zero the joint is separated (W-013). F_b replaces the tension in the interaction and is re-evaluated at every load multiplier k; preload itself does not scale. Clamp force is reported but no friction-slip capacity is claimed (N-005). With preload off the tension is T + Q.
      • Equilibrium closure (ΣR = F, ΣM = M for shear and axial) is checked on every calculation to a 1e-9 relative residual; a failure blocks the results.
      • Unit switching converts stored values in place to 12 significant figures (1 in = 25.4 mm, 1 lbf = 4.4482216152605 N). Imports keep the file's unit system.
